# Dovra – Document Reader & Converter (full description, EN)

Read and convert your documents privately.

Dovra is a private, offline-first document reader and converter for Android.
Open documents, preview them as clean structured text, and convert them to
the format you need — everything happens on your device. Your files are never
uploaded, and Dovra works without an account, without ads, and without
tracking.

## Read your documents

- Open TXT, Markdown, HTML, XML, RTF, DOC, DOCX, ODT, XLS, XLSX, ODS, CSV,
  TSV, PPT, PPTX, ODP, PDF, and EPUB.
- Clean structured preview: headings, paragraphs, lists, and tables.
- Open files from the system picker or directly from other apps.
- Safety first: Dovra checks magic-byte signatures and warns you if a file's
  contents don't match its extension.

## Convert honestly

Dovra only offers conversions its engine genuinely supports, each labelled:

- FULL — e.g. DOCX → TXT/HTML/RTF, XLSX → CSV/TSV/HTML, EPUB → TXT/HTML.
- SIMPLIFIED with an explicit note — e.g. DOCX → PDF (simplified layout),
  spreadsheets → PDF (table grid only), presentations → PDF/TXT (slide
  outline / text extraction only).

Unsupported pairs never appear as options. Live progress, cancellation, and
clear error messages are built in.

## Private by design

- 100% on-device processing — no cloud upload, no internet permission needed
  for reading or converting.
- No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Files are accessed only through Android's Storage Access Framework (the
  files and folders you explicitly pick).
- Save results with Save As to the folder you choose (default:
  Documents/Dovra), or share via the system share sheet.
- Conversion history stores names, formats, sizes, and timestamps only —
  never document contents. Temporary files are auto-deleted.

## Make it yours

- System / Light / Dark themes.
- Optional on-device history with per-item delete and clear-all.
- Free forever, developed independently. An optional Support section links to
  external donation pages in your browser.

Dovra: your documents stay on your device.
