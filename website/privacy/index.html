Dovra Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026.
App: Dovra – Document Reader & Converter (com.docuconvert.app)

Dovra is a private, offline-first document reader and converter for Android. This policy describes what the app does with your information, based on its actual implementation.

1. Short version

  • Your documents are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, never sent to a cloud API, and never shared by the app except when you explicitly choose to share or save a file.
  • Dovra contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking.
  • Dovra requests no internet permission for document processing.
  • No account is required, and no account information is collected.
  • The app stores only local settings and conversion metadata (file names, formats, sizes, timestamps) — never document contents.

2. Document processing (local only)

  1. You pick files through Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework) or share them from another app. Dovra reads only the file you selected.
  2. Reading, format detection, and conversion run locally with on-device libraries. The app performs no network requests during document processing, and external resources referenced inside a document (e.g. remote images) are not fetched.
  3. Temporary conversion files live in the app's private cache and are deleted after conversion succeeds, fails, is cancelled, or times out (2-minute limit). Orphaned files older than 24 hours are removed automatically.

Document contents never leave your device unless you tap Share / Open / Save As, in which case Android hands the file to the destination you chose.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Settings (appearance, output folder, history toggle, auto-open) — your choices only.
  • Conversion history (optional; can be disabled or cleared) — file names, formats, sizes, timestamps. Metadata only, never file bytes.
  • Temporary files — working copies during conversion; never backed up; auto-deleted.

SharedPreferences backup is excluded by the app's data-extraction rules, and document files themselves are never backed up by the app.

4. Permissions

Dovra declares zero runtime permissions: no internet, no broad storage access. File access works through the Storage Access Framework — the system grants access to exactly the files and folders you select. Sharing uses Android's FileProvider, which exposes only the app's cache converted/, shared/, temp/ paths and outputs/ — never databases or preferences.

5. What Dovra does not do

  • No advertising or advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs, or remote configuration.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or identity collection. No location access.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — there is no data pipeline to sell from.

6. Third-party libraries

Dovra is built with open-source libraries (fully listed in the app under Settings → About → Open Source Licenses). They run locally as parsers and UI frameworks; none is configured to transmit your documents anywhere.

7. Support / donations (external, optional)

The in-app Support section links to Buy Me a Coffee and Saweria. Tapping a link opens it in your own external browser. Donating is optional, takes place on those sites under their own policies, and no payment data flows through the app.

8. Retention and deletion

Temporary files are deleted as described above. History and settings persist until you delete or clear them, clear app data, or uninstall the app — which removes all app-private data. Because Dovra has no servers or accounts, there is nothing else to request.

9. Children

Dovra collects no personal data from anyone, including children. There is no age-gated content and no communication feature.

10. Changes

If Dovra's behavior changes, this policy will be updated with a revised effective date, reflected in the app listing with the corresponding update.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Dovra, contact the developer at bookirwan@gmail.com. You may also use the support channel on the app's store listing.
